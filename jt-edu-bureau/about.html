About

A registry, not a directory

JT Education Bureau Private Limited connects students and families across Delhi NCR with tutors who have actually been checked — not just listed.

Most tutor-finding is either word of mouth — slow, and limited to whoever your neighbour happens to know — or an open directory, where anyone can list themselves with no real check on who they are. JT sits between the two: every tutor's credentials and experience are reviewed before they're added to the registry, so what you're browsing is checked, not just claimed.

We currently work with a few hundred students and several dozen tutors across Delhi NCR, covering school subjects from primary level through Class 12 boards, plus foundation and competitive-exam coaching. It's small enough that a real person — not an algorithm — is behind every match.
