How JT works

Two registries, one coordinator in between

Students and tutors go through different steps to join — a coordinator sits in the middle of both, matching one to the other by hand.

For students & parents

01

Submit a request

Subject, class or exam, board, locality and preferred mode — a two-minute form, no account needed.

02

Get a shortlist

A coordinator checks the registry, confirms availability with matching tutors, and calls you with two or three options.

03

Start classes

Choose a tutor, agree a schedule directly, and begin — at home or online.

For tutors

01

Apply

Share your subjects, qualifications, experience and the areas you can cover.

02

Get verified

A short call to confirm your credentials and teaching experience before you're added to the live registry.

03

Get matched

When a student request fits your subjects and area, your coordinator calls you first.

Ready to start?

Request a tutor or apply to teach — both take under two minutes.
