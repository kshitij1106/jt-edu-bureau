How JT works

Two registries, one coordinator in between

Students and tutors go through different steps to join — a coordinator sits in the middle of both, matching one to the other by hand.

For students & parents

01

Create a free account

Name, class, board and locality — saved once, so marking interest anywhere on the site is a single click.

02

Browse and mark interest

Filter the tutor directory by subject, region or mode, then click "I'm interested" on any tutor that fits.

03

They follow up

The tutor sees your basic details in their dashboard and reaches out directly to arrange classes.

For tutors

01

Register

Create a tutor account and fill in your subjects, qualifications, experience and rate.

02

Get verified

A coordinator reviews your profile before it appears on the public directory.

03

Hear from students

When a student marks interest, you'll see their details in your dashboard and can follow up directly.
